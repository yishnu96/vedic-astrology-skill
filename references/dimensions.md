# The 14 Life Dimensions — Reading Recipes

`answer-question.js` classifies a question into 1-3 of these dimensions and runs each recipe. The machine-readable form is `lib/dimensions.js`; this file is the human reference. Sources: BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Jaimini Sutras, plus Raman / K.N. Rao / P.V.R. Narasimha Rao. Where classical and modern practice differ, classical (Parashari) wins by default.

**Universal reading order** (every life dimension follows it): D1 promise → main varga confirmation → D27/D30 durability → D60 karmic depth → dasha + transit timing.

---

## 1. CAREER & PROFESSION
- **Houses:** 10th (primary); 6th, 11th (secondary); 2nd, 1st, 7th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Sun, Saturn (primary); Mercury, Jupiter, Mars, Venus (secondary); Amatyakaraka (Jaimini).
- **Main varga:** D10. **Support:** D1, D9, D24. **Durability:** D27, D30. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 10th lord placement/dignity/aspects; planets in/aspecting 10th (D1+D10); does D10 lagna+10th confirm D1; Sun & Saturn condition; Amatyakaraka; Arudha of 10th (A10) for public perception; 6th (service) vs 7th (business).
- **Yogas:** Panch Mahapurusha, Dharma-Karmadhipati, Raja, Vipreet Raja, Budhaditya, Neecha Bhanga, Saraswati, Sasa, Kemadruma, Adhi.
- **Dasha:** strongest when active lord is 10th lord, 10th occupant, Sun, Saturn, or Amatyakaraka.
- **Transit:** Saturn over 10th/10th lord (restructuring); Jupiter over 10th/11th/2nd (expansion); Rahu over 10th (sudden shift); Sade-sati.
- **Astrocartography:** if location/foreign-job/relocation mentioned.
- **Confidence:** D10 birth-time sensitive (~3-5 min). Flag if accuracy worse than ±15min.

## 2. WEALTH & MONEY
- **Houses:** 2nd, 11th (primary); 5th, 9th (secondary); 1st, 10th, 8th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Jupiter (primary); Venus, Mercury, Moon (secondary).
- **Main varga:** D2 Hora. **Support:** D1, D9, D11. **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 2nd & 11th lords; Jupiter's condition; planets in 2/5/9/11; which Hora (Sun's = active wealth, Moon's = passive/inherited); any two of 1/2/5/9/11 lords linked → Dhana yoga; sign of 11th (fixed = stable income).
- **Yogas:** Dhana (10 combos), Lakshmi, Maha Lakshmi, Chandra-Mangal, Vasumati, Gajakesari, Adhi, Sunapha/Anapha/Durudhura, Kemadruma, Daridra, Vipreet Raja, Neecha Bhanga.
- **Dasha:** dasha of 2nd/11th/5th/9th lord or Jupiter; Jupiter's own dasha generally favourable.
- **Transit:** Jupiter over 2/5/9/11; Saturn over 2/11 (restricted); Rahu over 2/11 (unstable).
- **Confidence:** D2 robust; standard tolerance.

## 3. MARRIAGE & PARTNERSHIP
- **Houses:** 7th (primary); 2nd, 8th (secondary); 5th, 11th, 12th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Venus — for **both genders** per BPHS (primary); Jupiter (husband significator, female chart), Mars, Rahu (secondary); Darakaraka (Jaimini).
- **Main varga:** D9 — the marriage chart itself, not just a strength check. **Support:** D1, D7, D12. **Durability:** D27, D30. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 7th lord; Venus strength/combustion; Mangal Dosha (Mars from Lagna AND Moon AND Venus); **D9 ascendant + D9 7th house + D9 7th lord**; Darakaraka in D1/D9; UpaPada Lagna (UL) and 7th from UL; 8th house for longevity.
- **Yogas:** Mangal Dosha (+ 5 cancellation rules), Gajakesari, Paap Kartari / Shubha Kartari on 7th, Vipreet Raja, Kemadruma.
- **Dasha:** 7th lord, Venus, 7th occupants, Darakaraka, 2nd/11th lord. Most reliable: Venus or 7th-lord dasha.
- **Transit:** Jupiter over 7th/7th lord/Venus (strongest); Saturn over 7th; Rahu-Ketu on 7th axis.
- **Confidence:** D9 moderately sensitive (~5-7 min). Flag if worse than ±15min.

## 4. CHILDREN & FERTILITY
- **Houses:** 5th (primary); 9th (secondary, 3rd child by Bhavat Bhavam); 7th, 11th, 2nd (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Jupiter (primary); Sun & Venus (male chart), Moon & Mars (female chart); Putrakaraka (Jaimini).
- **Main varga:** D7. **Support:** D1, D9. **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 5th lord; Jupiter; 5th occupants; **D7 lagna + 5th house + 5th lord**; Putrakaraka; 5th from Moon; 5th from Jupiter; **Beeja Sphuta** (Sun+Venus, male) and **Kshetra Sphuta** (Jupiter+Moon+Mars, female) — `extras.fertilitySphutas`; fertile (water) vs barren (air) signs in 5th.
- **Yogas:** Pitru Dosha, Kemadruma. Note: 5th lord with Mercury/Venus/Saturn can obstruct Jupiter's putra-karaka role.
- **Dasha:** Jupiter, 5th lord, 5th occupants, Putrakaraka. Jupiter mahadasha most reliable.
- **Transit:** Jupiter through 5th or over natal Jupiter; Saturn over 5th/5th lord (delay).
- **Confidence:** D7 sensitivity ~9-10 min. Flag if worse than ±30min.

## 5. HEALTH & VITALITY
- **Houses:** 1st, 6th (primary); 8th, 12th (secondary); 2nd, 4th, 5th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Sun, Moon (primary); Mars, Saturn, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Rahu, Ketu — each maps to body systems.
- **Main varga:** D1 itself (no dedicated health varga). **Support:** D6, D9. **Durability:** D27, D30. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** Lagna lord (constitution); 6th house/lord (disease); 8th (longevity band); 12th (hospitalisation, mental); Moon's condition (mental health); affliction patterns; body-part mapping by sign/house.
- **Yogas:** Kemadruma, Chandra-Mangal in dusthana, Vish, Shapit, Pitru Dosha, Kaal Sarp, Vipreet Raja (counterintuitive survival).
- **Dasha:** 6th/8th/12th lord, malefics in 6/8/12.
- **Transit:** Saturn over Lagna/Moon/Sun; Mars through 1/6/8; eclipses on Lagna axis or Moon; Sade-sati.
- **Confidence:** **CRITICAL ETHICS** — never predict death timing or terminal diagnoses. Frame as "vulnerable periods supported by preventive care." Refuse exact death timing even if asked.

## 6. EDUCATION & LEARNING
- **Houses:** 4th, 5th (primary); 9th, 2nd (secondary); 12th, 8th, 3rd (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Mercury, Jupiter (primary); Moon, Sun, Saturn (secondary); Mars/Venus/Rahu/Ketu for subject inclination.
- **Main varga:** D24. **Support:** D1, D9. **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 4th lord (school), 5th lord (graduation), 9th lord (post-grad/PhD); Mercury & Jupiter; **D24 lagna, 5th, 9th, 12th**.
- **Yogas:** Saraswati, Budhaditya, Gajakesari, Bhadra, Hamsa.
- **Dasha:** Mercury, Jupiter, 4th/5th/9th lord. Rahu → foreign/unconventional learning. Saturn → long disciplined courses.
- **Transit:** Jupiter over 5th/9th/natal Mercury; Saturn over 4th/9th; eclipses on 4/5/9.
- **Astrocartography:** if foreign-education mentioned.
- **Confidence:** D24 highly sensitive (~5-6 min, per K.N. Rao). Flag if worse than ±15min.

## 7. PROPERTY, HOME & ASSETS
- **Houses:** 4th (primary); 2nd, 11th, 8th (secondary); 12th, 3rd, 1st (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Mars (Bhumi karaka), Moon (primary); Venus, Mercury, Saturn (secondary).
- **Main varga:** D4. **Support:** D1, D9, D16 (vehicles/luxuries). **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 4th lord; Mars & Moon; **D4 lagna + 4th house**; planets in 4th (D1+D4); 11th-4th link (gains via property); 8th-4th link (inheritance).
- **Yogas:** Malavya, Vipreet Raja.
- **Dasha:** 4th lord, Mars, Moon, 4th occupants, 11th lord. Venus dasha → luxury home/vehicle.
- **Transit:** Jupiter over 4th (gain); Saturn over 4th (burden); Rahu over 4th (foreign/sudden move); Mars over 4th (disputes).
- **Astrocartography:** strong trigger — any buying/selling/relocating question.
- **Confidence:** D4 mild sensitivity (~15 min). Standard tolerance.

## 8. RELATIONSHIPS, LOVE & SOCIAL
Distinct from Marriage — pre-marital romance, friendships, social network.
- **Houses:** 5th (romance), 11th (friends/network) (primary); 7th, 3rd (secondary); 8th, 12th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Venus (primary); Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Rahu (secondary).
- **Main varga:** D9 (committed), D1 (casual). **Support:** D1, D7. **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 5th lord vs 7th lord (love-to-marriage flow); Venus; 5th-7th lord interchange (love-marriage indicator); Rahu in 5th/7th; 8th (secret bonds); 11th (social satisfaction).
- **Yogas:** Chandra-Mangal, Kemadruma, Adhi.
- **Dasha:** Venus, 5th lord, Rahu (romance); 11th lord (friendship); interlinked 5th-7th lord dasha → love marriage.
- **Transit:** Venus through 5th/7th; Jupiter through 5th; Rahu through 5th/7th.
- **Astrocartography:** if "love abroad" / "best city for romance".

## 9. FAMILY, PARENTS & SIBLINGS
- **Houses:** 4th (mother), 9th (father), 3rd (younger siblings), 11th (elder siblings) (primary); 2nd, 7th (secondary); 12th, 8th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Moon (mother), Sun (father), Mars (younger sibling), Jupiter (elder sibling); Pitrukaraka & Matrukaraka (Jaimini).
- **Main varga:** D12. **Support:** D1, D3 (siblings). **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** Sun (father), Moon (mother); 9th lord, 4th lord; **D12 lagna, 4th, 9th**; 3rd & 11th for siblings.
- **Yogas:** Pitru Dosha, Shapit Dosha; Sun-Saturn / Sun-node (father friction); Moon-Saturn / Moon-node (mother distance).
- **Dasha:** Sun/9th lord/Pitrukaraka (father); Moon/4th lord/Matrukaraka (mother); Mars or Jupiter, 3rd/11th lord (siblings).
- **Transit:** Saturn over natal Sun / natal Moon; eclipses on Sun/Moon (use cautiously).
- **Confidence:** D12 sensitivity ~10 min. **Never give exact timing for parental health** — only vulnerable periods supported by care.

## 10. TRAVEL & FOREIGN CONNECTIONS
- **Houses:** 12th (primary); 9th, 7th (secondary); 3rd, 4th, 8th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Rahu (primary); Moon, Jupiter, Saturn, Ketu (secondary).
- **Main varga:** D1; add D10 (foreign career), D9 (foreign spouse), D24 (foreign education). **Support:** D12. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 12th lord; 9th lord; Rahu's house; 9th-12th lord connection (strong settlement indicator); 4th affliction (uprooting).
- **Yogas:** 12th lord in 9th / 9th lord in 12th; Rahu in 9th/12th; 4th lord in 12th; 7th lord in 12th (foreign spouse); 10th lord in 12th (foreign career); Moon-Rahu; Vipreet Raja.
- **Dasha:** Rahu, 12th lord, 9th lord, 9th/12th occupants. Rahu mahadasha most common foreign window.
- **Transit:** Jupiter through 9th/12th; Rahu through 9th/12th/7th; Saturn through 12th.
- **Astrocartography:** **ALWAYS triggered** — this dimension relies on it most. Return global planetary lines with cities and effects.

## 11. SPIRITUALITY & DHARMA
- **Houses:** 9th, 12th (primary); 5th, 8th (secondary); 4th (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Jupiter (Guru-karaka), Ketu (Moksha-karaka) (primary); Venus (bhakti), Saturn (karma yoga), Moon (meditation); Atmakaraka (Jaimini — the soul's primary lesson).
- **Main varga:** D20. **Support:** D1 (moksha trikona 4/8/12), D9 (Karakamsha = AK's sign in D9). **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 9th & 12th lords; Jupiter & Ketu; moksha trikona (4/8/12) strength; dharma trikona (1/5/9) strength; **D20 lagna, 5th, 9th, 12th**; Atmakaraka & its D9 placement.
- **Yogas:** Moksha Yoga, Pravrajya Yoga, Kemadruma (forces inward turn), Hamsa.
- **Dasha:** Jupiter, Ketu, 9th/12th/5th lord. Ketu mahadasha → forced detachment. Jupiter → guru-finding.
- **Transit:** Jupiter over 9th/12th; Saturn over 12th; Rahu through 9th; Ketu through 1st/9th.
- **Confidence:** D20 sensitivity ~6 min. Standard.

## 12. OBSTACLES, MISFORTUNE & ENEMIES
- **Houses:** 6th, 8th, 12th (primary); 3rd, 11th (secondary); 1st (tertiary).
- **Karakas:** Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, 6th lord.
- **Main varga:** D30. **Support:** D1, D6. **Durability:** D27. **Karmic:** D60.
- **Check:** 6th/8th/12th lords; malefics in dusthanas; **D30 lagna + 6/8/12**; Ketu (sudden events); Saturn affliction patterns; Rahu's house.
- **Yogas:** **Vipreet Raja Yoga** (paradoxically protective), Kaal Sarp, Pitru Dosha, Shapit, Guru Chandala, Angarak, Vish, Daridra, Kemadruma, Paap Kartari.
- **Event-style workflow** (e.g. "accident risk?"): (1) D1 markers — Mars/Ketu/Saturn on/aspecting Lagna/Lagna lord/6/8? (2) if yes, which dasha activates? (3) running or upcoming in 2-3 yrs? (4) cross-check live transits; (5) answer with the dasha window + transit trigger + **mitigation**, never a bare yes/no. `event_analysis` in the reading does steps 1-4.
- **Dasha:** 6th/8th/12th lord, malefics in those houses. Vipreet Raja lords' dasha → trouble brings success.
- **Transit:** Saturn over Lagna/Moon/Sun; Mars+Saturn or Mars+Rahu on natal afflicted points; eclipses on Lagna axis; Sade-sati.
- **Confidence:** **CRITICAL ETHICS** — never frame as fate, always pair with mitigation, never predict specific accidents/deaths as certain. Use "elevated risk" language.

## 13. DEEP KARMA & PAST LIFE
- **Houses:** 5th (purvapunya), 12th (past-life liabilities) (primary); 9th, 8th (secondary).
- **Karakas:** Atmakaraka (the soul, primary lesson); Ketu (past-life knowledge), Rahu (this-life direction), Saturn (karmic burden), Jupiter (dharmic merit).
- **Main varga:** D60. **Support:** D1 (lagna + 5/9/12), D9 (Karakamsha).
- **Check:** **REQUIRES `birth_time_accuracy = exact`** — otherwise the orchestrator returns a refusal-with-warning and gives no specifics. AK & its nakshatra; AK in D9 (Karakamsha lagna); D1 5th & 12th; **D60 lagna + key houses**; Saturn & Ketu; Rahu-Ketu axis.
- **Yogas:** Shapit, Pitru Dosha, Kaal Sarp, Moksha.
- **Dasha:** AK's mahadasha is the most karmically intense.
- **Transit:** Rahu-Ketu axis transits (~every 18 months).
- **Confidence:** **ABSOLUTE** — never run if birth time is not exact. Even a 5-minute error makes D60 unreliable.

## 14. TIMING & LIFE-PERIODS (meta-dimension)
No house/varga setup of its own — a "when" router applied across other dimensions.
- **Check:** current Maha/Antar/Pratyantar; Sade-sati status; Jupiter gochar from natal Moon (2/5/7/9/11 auspicious; 4/8/12 challenging); major upcoming transits & dasha changes (2-3 yrs); eclipse points.
- **Output:** a timeline view in buckets — 0-3mo, 3-12mo, 12-24mo, 24-36mo — each with active dasha, key transits, and flavour. If the user also asked a life-dimension question, overlay this timeline on that dimension's reading.
